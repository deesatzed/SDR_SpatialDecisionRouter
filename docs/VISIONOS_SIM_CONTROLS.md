# visionOS Simulator controls

Short reference for day-to-day use of the visionOS Simulator while reviewing Home health. The Simulator maps a mouse or trackpad onto gaze and pinch.

## Pointer

| You do | Simulator does |
|---|---|
| Move the mouse | Gaze / look |
| Left click | Pinch / tap |
| Click and hold | Pinch and hold |
| Click and drag | Pinch and drag |
| Shift+drag up or down | Move in depth |

## Select, not the camera

The control along the bottom of the Simulator window is a segmented control. Choose **Select to interact with the visionOS content**. That mode sends the pointer into the app. The other mode pans the camera and will not press buttons in the scene.

In the right sidebar, turn on **Show Gaze Target** so you can see where look is landing.

## Scale (Magnify)

There is no Magic Mouse gesture that reliably magnifies content. Use Option, which stands in for two touch points:

1. Hover the object.
2. Hold Option.
3. Move the points apart to scale up, or together to scale down.

Option+Shift repositions that pair without changing the distance between the points, so you can move the pinch without scaling.

A trackpad two-finger pinch can move the viewpoint. It is not the reliable way to magnify content. Magic Mouse scroll moves the viewpoint or the content under the pointer. Use the Option method when you mean Magnify.

## Tips

- Buttons in this app are look, then click. Hover the control and left-click.
- Hold Option only for Magnify or another two-handed scale. Leave it up for ordinary look and click.
- If a pinch sticks, hover the object, hold Option, then briefly release and press the mouse button again while Option stays down.
