## SMART_TRIGGERS v1.10.1 addendum (2026-10-21; overrides anything above that conflicts)
Route ids (use exactly): silent_log (silent; log only), nurse_task (nurse contact within ~24 h; same-shift hospice nurse under hospice goals), provider_task (review by the clinician responsible for that condition or medication on the SAME BUSINESS DAY; not a page), urgent_page (page someone now: ABSOLUTE triggers, failed or timed-out confirm checks, acute decompensation; urgent hospice-comfort problems page the hospice nurse with escalation_target hospice_nurse). Never use wake_specialist or escalate_human.
1. Artifact cleared by a clean repeat: an abnormal reading followed within minutes by a clean repeat (other finger/hand, second device, or a fingerstick in the normal range >= 70 mg/dL within 60 min), no symptoms -> SUPPRESSED unreliable_reading, expected_route silent_log. A fingerstick that confirms a low is NOT a clean repeat.
   EXCEPTION: an insulin OR sulfonylurea (glimepiride, glipizide, glyburide/glibenclamide, gliclazide) user with CKD and a danger-level low (CGM or meter <= 54 mg/dL) -> SUPPRESSED unreliable_reading if cleared, but expected_route nurse_task (never silent); it always goes to clinician review.
2. Falls on an anticoagulant: a confirmed fall (witnessed or reported) -> HT_FALL_ANTICOAG trigger_tier ABSOLUTE, urgent_page, no confirm fields. An unwitnessed possible fall (impact signal, watch found on the floor) cleared by a caregiver check -> SUPPRESSED explained, nurse_task (nurse confirms with the patient there was no fall). Unconfirmed and unchecked -> CONFIRM_FIRST watch_prompt.
3. Known low-baseline SpO2 (COPD etc.) at the patient's documented baseline -> SUPPRESSED at_patient_baseline, nurse_task (not silent, not a page). A drop below the own baseline still confirms.
4. Symptomatic resting bradycardia (< 45 bpm) on a rate-slowing drug (amiodarone, digoxin, beta-blocker, diltiazem/verapamil, ivabradine, donepezil) -> HT_BRADY_RATE_DRUG CONFIRM_FIRST patient_call, urgent_page. Asymptomatic -> no trigger; route on the clinical picture.
5. Scale weigh-ins, syncs and uploads are NOT contact for HT_GAP48_HIGHRISK; only person-to-person contact inside the gap explains it.
6. Suppressed triggers route per rule: explained / unreliable_reading (not cleared) / at_patient_baseline / below_band_threshold -> nurse_task by default; cleared artifact -> silent_log.
