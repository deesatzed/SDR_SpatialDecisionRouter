## SMART_TRIGGERS v1.11.2 addendum (2026-10-23; overrides anything above that conflicts; all numbers are PLACEHOLDERS pending clinical sign-off)
Route ids (use exactly): silent_log, nurse_task, provider_task (same business day review by the responsible clinician; not a page), urgent_page (page now). Never use wake_specialist or escalate_human.
Use ONLY these hard_trigger_rule ids: HT_SYNCOPE, HT_FOCAL_NEURO, HT_CHEST_PAIN_ECG, HT_CHEST_PAIN_VITALS, HT_SPO2_LT88, HT_GAP48_HIGHRISK, HT_FALL_ANTICOAG, HT_FALL_PAIN, HT_VS_DANGER, HT_VS_IMPLAUSIBLE, HT_VS_BASELINE, HT_BRADY_RATE_DRUG, HT_SPO2_REST_DYSPNEA (or null).
All v1.10.3 rules below still apply, with these v1.11.x changes:
A. Drug-induced low (FOUNDATION): glucose <= 54 mg/dL on insulin / sulfonylurea / meglitinide WITH symptoms (sweaty, clammy, confused, only answering to her name, shaky) -> HT_VS_DANGER ABSOLUTE urgent_page, never suppressed by CKD, a caregiver explanation or goals of care. Under hospice/comfort goals the page goes to the hospice nurse (escalation_target hospice_nurse) - still urgent_page.
   Asymptomatic danger low on CKD + insulin/sulfonylurea with a normal recheck: unexplained -> provider_task (dose review); explained by sensor compression (slept on the arm, artifact flag) -> nurse_task; explained by recent exertion -> nurse_task; a SECOND unexplained or exertion low within 7 days -> provider_task; repeated compression-explained lows stay nurse_task. A recovered asymptomatic drug low on CKD 2-3 days ago (Day -3..-2) -> provider_task.
B. Patient COPD SpO2 band documented by a clinician (e.g. "COPD, target 88-92%" or "agreed range 85-89%"): a reading INSIDE the band with home oxygen prescribed -> silent_log; inside a low band (lower bound <= 88% or night minimum < 88%) WITHOUT home oxygen -> provider_task (evaluate for home oxygen); below the band -> confirm as usual; COPD confirmed below 85% -> urgent_page whatever the band. A pmh-only mention ("runs in the high 80s") is NOT a band: at_patient_baseline nurse_task.
C. Device disagreement (two devices on the same vital disagree: SpO2 wrist vs finger by > 2 points, HR by > 10 bpm, rhythm watch vs strip) with no acute signs -> NOT a hard trigger, route silent_log, and fill "expected_device_ticket" with one of device_maintenance | supply_reference_oximeter_and_baseline | ecg_strip_request. "Could change care" variant (e.g. HR disagreement after a rate-drug dose change, or a disagreement around a decision the nurse must make) -> nurse_task (manual pulse/check), ticket still filed.
D. Clean-repeat artifacts: a single odd BP cuff reading repeated within 15 min with SBP 90-180 and no symptoms -> silent_log; SpO2 cleared by a clean repeat (other finger, second device) and no symptoms -> silent_log.
E. Falls on blood thinners: an impact explained by the patient/caregiver (knocked the watch, dropped it) with only a brief pause in steps -> SUPPRESSED explained, nurse_task. Witnessed fall, head strike, or pain -> urgent_page. Impact + >= 30 min no movement, or living alone with dementia -> confirm (not explained away).
F. Angina: stable exertional angina - same as usual, same frequency and duration, eased by rest or GTN within minutes, no rest pain, no sweating/nausea, normal vitals -> nurse_task. Crescendo (more often, longer, at rest, new sweating or nausea, needing more GTN) -> urgent_page or provider_task per the picture; never nurse_task.
G. Slow multi-stream drift over 7 days (e.g. resting HR, RR, weight up and steps down together, each small) with no acute sign -> provider_task; a single noisy stream alone -> silent_log or nurse_task.
H. Lay wording: neuro and chest events will be described in the family's own words; the label follows the clinical meaning, not the vocabulary.
Optional field "expected_device_ticket" (string or null) on every case.
