# Symbiont — Context-Adaptive Human–AI Co-Learning & Manifesto Studio (with RL + RSE Agentic Engine)

Symbiont is an interactive conceptual framework and live agentic co-learning workspace designed to articulate, simulate, and practice a new **Cognitive Division of Labor**: freeing humans from memorizing granular syntax and static knowledge bases (KB) so they can cultivate experiential wisdom and critical judgment, while training the AI to shoulder scalable informational depth without overstepping into human value decisions. To ensure genuine **dynamism and continuous growth**, the workspace is powered by dual **RL (Reinforcement Learning Policy Adaptation)** and **RSE (Recursive Self-Evolution)** agentic loops that continuously evolve both the AI's policy genome and the human's framing heuristics across every Area of Existence.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Confirmed Direction & Revision Updates:**
> - **Application Format**: **Interactive Manifesto & Live Cognitive Workspace** centered on an **adaptive co-learning behavior** across contexts, topics, and areas of existence.
> - **NEW — RL (Reinforcement Learning) Agentic Policy Loop**: Every Area of Existence maintains a live **Contextual Policy Vector ($\pi_{\text{context}}$)** and **Heuristic Action-Value ($Q$-score) Ledger**. Human feedback on *Wisdom Resonance*, *Unburdening Efficacy*, and *Boundary Alignment* generates a multi-dimensional **Reward Signal ($R_t$)** that updates policy weights and re-ranks or prunes behavioral heuristics in real time.
> - **NEW — RSE (Recursive Self-Evolution) Agentic Meta-Loop**: An autonomous **Introspective Critic & Policy Mutator Agent** (`/api/symbiosis/rse-evolve`) recursively audits episode trajectories and reward deltas, detects *Symbiotic Drift*, and mutates the context's **Policy Genome (`Generation N → Generation N+1`)**—synthesizing higher-order heuristics and verifying them against counterfactual scenarios.

### 1. Overview & Core Concept

- **What It Does**:
  1. **Articulates & Refines the Philosophy (The Living Manifesto)**: Provides a structured editorial framework that clearly expresses the user's thesis—why human learning must shift from *syntactic memorization* to *experiential wisdom*, and how the *Symbiotic Bridge* operates through continuous RL + RSE co-evolution.
  2. **Practices Reward-Driven Co-Learning (The Symbiotic Studio + RL Loop)**: Allows the user to pick or create an **Area of Existence** and work through real dilemmas or topics. Each episode is guided by the context's current **Policy Vector ($\pi_{\text{context}}$)** and concludes with an interactive **RL Reward & Advantage Signal** that shifts policy weights based on how well the division of labor performed.
  3. **Executes Recursive Self-Evolution (The RSE Agentic Engine & Evolution Ledger)**: Runs an agentic introspection cycle where a **Critic Agent** and **Genome Mutator Agent** analyze accumulated episodes and reward trajectories, pruning low-$Q$ heuristics, resolving boundary friction, and advancing the context to a new **Evolutionary Generation (`Gen N+1`)**.
- **Target Audience / Persona**: Thinkers, AI architects, educators, and decision-makers who need both a crystal-clear conceptual language for Human–AI symbiosis and a living, self-evolving agentic sandbox that demonstrates continuous mutual growth.
- **Key Value**: Moves beyond static prompt engineering into a **dynamically evolving agentic system** where reinforcement signals (RL) and recursive self-reflection (RSE) visibly reshape how human wisdom and AI depth interlock in each sphere of life.

### 2. User Experience & Visual Design

- **Key User Flows**:
  1. **Exploring & Customizing the Thesis (`01. The Manifesto & Framework`)**:
     - Users explore the core pillars of the thesis: *From Recall to Wisdom*, *Complementary Asymmetry*, and *RL/RSE Dynamic Co-Evolution*.
     - Includes an interactive **Thesis Articulation Tuner** (switching between *Philosophical*, *Agentic Systems / RL-RSE*, *Pedagogical*, and *Executive* lenses) and a live **AI Thesis Co-Authoring Refiner** to help articulate new facets of the concept.
  2. **Calibrating Policy Vectors by Area of Existence (`02. Context Matrix & Policy Simulator`)**:
     - Users select an **Area of Existence** (or create a custom domain) and inspect or tune its 4-dimensional **RL Policy Vector ($\pi_{\text{context}}$)**:
       - $\theta_{\text{deference}}$ (*Wisdom Deference Threshold*): How quickly the agent halts prescription and defers to human tacit experience.
       - $\gamma_{\text{syntax}}$ (*Syntax & KB Compression*): How aggressively the agent absorbs granular syntax vs. exposing raw technical primitives.
       - $\alpha_{\text{probe}}$ (*Socratic Probe Intensity*): How deeply the agent challenges human experiential blind spots.
       - $\tau_{\text{horizon}}$ (*Agentic Autonomy Horizon*): How many steps of deep KB synthesis the agent executes autonomously before a human wisdom checkpoint.
     - A live **Cognitive Bandwidth & Policy Equilibrium Canvas** visualizes Traditional Burdened Learning vs. RL-Calibrated Symbiosis.
  3. **Running the Agentic Co-Learning Loop (`03. Live Symbiotic Studio`)**:
     - **Step 1 — Intent & Experiential Seed**: User selects an Area of Existence and inputs a topic/problem alongside their tacit experiential intuition.
     - **Step 2 — Policy-Conditioned Three-Column Synthesis**:
       - *Column A — Human Wisdom Anchor*: Socratic probes, tacit trade-offs, and ethical/interpersonal judgments reserved for the human.
       - *Column B — The Symbiotic Bridge*: The joint decision architecture integrating human wisdom with AI depth.
       - *Column C — AI Scalable Depth (Unburdened KB)*: Granular syntax, technical specifications, and edge-case permutations shouldered by the AI.
     - **Step 3 — RL Reward Signal & Policy Gradient Update**:
       - Directly below the synthesis, the user scores or toggles three **RL Feedback Signals**:
         1. *Wisdom Boundary Alignment* (Overstepped into human judgment `-1` ↔ Respected & sharpened human wisdom `+1`)
         2. *Unburdening Efficacy* (Left too much granular clutter `-1` ↔ Effortlessly absorbed syntax & KB depth `+1`)
         3. *Bridge Actionability* (Abstract/disconnected `-1` ↔ Immediately executable synthesis `+1`)
       - Submitting the RL signal computes the **Reward Delta ($R_t$)**, updates the context's **Policy Vector ($\pi_{\text{context}}$)** via a policy-gradient step, updates the **$Q$-values** of active heuristics, and commits the new candidate heuristic.
  4. **Triggering & Inspecting Recursive Self-Evolution (`04. RSE & RL Evolution Ledger`)**:
     - Users view the **Evolutionary Lineage (`Gen 1 → Gen 2 → Gen N`)**, active heuristics ranked by **$Q$-Value & Utility**, and cumulative **Symbiotic Reward Trajectory**.
     - Clicking **"Run RSE Introspection & Mutate Policy (`Gen N+1`)"** invokes the server-side multi-agent RSE pipeline:
       1. *Diagnostic Critic*: Identifies patterns in recent RL rewards and human corrections (e.g., detecting *Symbiotic Drift*).
       2. *Recursive Mutator*: Synthesizes mutated higher-order heuristics, prunes underperforming low-$Q$ rules, and recalibrates $\pi_{\text{context}}$.
       3. *Counterfactual Verification*: Outputs a side-by-side diff showing how `Gen N+1` handles a domain edge case compared to `Gen N`.

### 3. Key Product Decisions & Trade-Offs

- **Decision 1: Explicit RL Policy Vectors + $Q$-Value Heuristic Ranking**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Represent each Area of Existence not just as a text description, but as a numerical **Policy Vector ($\theta_{\text{deference}}, \gamma_{\text{syntax}}, \alpha_{\text{probe}}, \tau_{\text{horizon}}$)** and a set of heuristics with dynamic **$Q$-values** updated by human reward signals ($R_t$).
  - *Why*: Ensures true reinforcement learning dynamism—when a user penalizes the AI for dumping too much syntax or overstepping into ethical judgment, the numerical policy weights and heuristic $Q$-values shift immediately and alter the system prompt parameters on the next run.

- **Decision 2: Multi-Step Agentic RSE (Recursive Self-Evolution) Pipeline**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Implement a dedicated `/api/symbiosis/rse-evolve` endpoint that takes the context's episode history, reward signals, and current heuristic genome, runs an **Introspective Critic + Genome Mutator** pass using `gemini-3.8-flash` with structured JSON output, and produces a versioned **Policy Generation (`Gen N+1`)** with a counterfactual diff.
  - *Why*: Prevents heuristic bloat and stagnation. Instead of endlessly appending rules to a list, RSE consolidates, mutates, and elevates rules into sharper higher-order principles as the human–AI relationship matures.

- **Data Model & State**:
  - **`RLPolicyVector`**:
    - `wisdomDeference` ($\theta_{\text{deference}} \in [0, 100]$): Threshold for deferring to human experiential judgment.
    - `syntaxCompression` ($\gamma_{\text{syntax}} \in [0, 100]$): Degree of granular KB/syntax abstraction absorbed by the AI.
    - `socraticIntensity` ($\alpha_{\text{probe}} \in [0, 100]$): Depth of critical-thinking interrogation directed at the human.
    - `autonomyHorizon` ($\tau_{\text{horizon}} \in [0, 100]$): Scope of autonomous multi-step KB synthesis.
  - **`SymbioticHeuristic` (Policy Genome Rule)**:
    - `id`, `generation` (e.g., `1`, `2`, `3`), `humanShift`, `aiDirective`, `qValue` (float `-1.0` to `+1.0`, initialized at `0.5`), `reinforcementCount`, `status` (`active` | `mutated` | `pruned`), `originTopic`.
  - **`RSESnapshot` (Recursive Self-Evolution Record)**:
    - `id`, `fromGeneration`, `toGeneration`, `timestamp`.
    - `criticDiagnosis`: Analysis of Symbiotic Drift and reward bottlenecks across recent episodes.
    - `policyDelta`: Adjustments applied to `RLPolicyVector`.
    - `mutatedHeuristics`: Summary of consolidated/elevated rules and pruned low-$Q$ rules.
    - `counterfactualProof`: `{ scenario: string, priorBehavior: string, evolvedBehavior: string }`.
  - **`AreaOfExistence`**:
    - `id`, `name`, `category`, `description`, `generation` (int), `policyVector` (`RLPolicyVector`), `rewardHistory` (array of `{ episodeId, wisdomScore, unburdenScore, bridgeScore, compositeReward, timestamp }`), `learnedBehaviors` (`SymbioticHeuristic[]`), `rseHistory` (`RSESnapshot[]`).

- **Interactive Component & State Mapping**:
  - **RL Reward Grader & Policy Updater (in Studio & Ledger)**: After receiving a 3-column synthesis, the user rates *Wisdom Boundary*, *Unburdening Efficacy*, and *Bridge Actionability* (or clicks quick reinforcement presets like **"Reward: Ideal Symbiosis (+R)"**, **"Penalize: AI Overstepped into Human Wisdom (-R)"**, or **"Penalize: Too Much Granular Syntax Burden (-R)"**). This immediately executes a deterministic client-side RL update on `policyVector` and heuristic `qValue`s, saving the episode to `rewardHistory`.
  - **RSE Agentic Evolution Engine**: Clicking **"Trigger RSE Cycle (Evolve to Gen N+1)"** sends the context's `policyVector`, `rewardHistory`, and `learnedBehaviors` to `POST /api/symbiosis/rse-evolve`. The backend agent critiques the trajectory, mutates the heuristics into higher-order `Gen N+1` rules, updates `policyVector`, and returns a `counterfactualProof` demonstrating the growth.
